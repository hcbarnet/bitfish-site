The Hollow County Angler's Guide

Everything the signs on the banks know, in one place. How the game works, what each water holds, and how to catch the fish that won't come easy.

How to play

Walk to the water's edge with the D-pad and face it. The action button reads CAST. Tap it, and wait: the bobber sits on the water while something decides whether it's hungry. When the text box says BITE!, tap anywhere — you have 2 seconds to set the hook. Miss it and the fish takes your bait and goes.

Once it's hooked, the fight begins.

The fight

Three bars. Tension climbs while you hold the reel and falls when you let go. Reeled in is how close the fish is. Line is how much of it is left.

Push tension into the red — it starts 48% of the way across the bar — and the line starts to fray. It never mends. A long fight with a lot of time in the red will part the line even if you never spike it. A short fight almost can't.

When the fish runs, holding on costs you tension fast and gains you almost nothing. Let go, let it run, and reel when it settles. Big fish that run hard are the ones that break lines.

Heavier line resists wear and recovers tension faster when you let off. If a water keeps breaking you off, the answer is almost always line before it's rod.

What's on the line

You fish one of two ways, and you choose in the BAG.

  • A lure is reusable and costs nothing per cast. Each lure appeals to certain fish — a spoon to one crowd, a frog to another. A fish that only eats bait will rarely chase hardware.
  • Bait on a hook is used up each cast. Fish that like that bait bite sooner and more often. A bigger hook holds bigger bait, and big fish notice.

Every species page below says what each fish takes. Matching it is the single biggest thing you control. Tap CHANGE in the bag to tie on a different lure or pick a bait from the box; buying bait at the shack puts it straight on the hook.

The day turns

A full day in Hollow County lasts about 25 real minutes. Dawn runs 5–9, day 9–17, dusk 17–21, and night the rest. Plenty of fish only move in one of those windows — the catfish and the gar after dark, the crappie at first and last light. If a water seems empty, it may just be the wrong hour. Check the species tables, or SLEEP from the bag: the bed skips straight to the next dawn, day, dusk or night. A sleep costs a short ad, and is free if you have bought Remove Ads.

Keep, sell, or tank

Every landed fish is logged in the Fishdex, with your personal best beside it. The first of a species pays 3×. Anything over its trophy weight can go in the Trophy Tank; everything else goes in the bucket and sells at the shack. Coins buy tackle, and tackle is what opens the harder water.

The tank starts with 4 slots and can be expanded three times, to 24. It can be decorated, too: some of what comes up on the line — an old boot, a glass float — can be kept for the gravel instead of sold, and the shack's TANK tab sells a castle, a chest and a bubbler. Four pieces stand at a time.

The shack also sells hats and shirts, one price each. Put them on from the WARDROBE in the bag.

Reading the species tables

  • Rarity — ★☆☆☆☆ common · ★★☆☆☆ uncommon · ★★★☆☆ rare · ★★★★☆ very rare · ★★★★★ legendary. Rarer fish bite less often.
  • Fight — one to five, from the fish alone. A five will break light line whatever you do.
  • Tackle — the lightest line that holds the species at its heaviest, and a rod to match. You can land it on less; you'll lose more.
